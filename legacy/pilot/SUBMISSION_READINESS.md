# Submission readiness checklist

## Before claiming this as a submission-ready SCI manuscript

- [ ] Replace pilot graphs with a documented benchmark suite of appropriate size and provenance.
- [ ] Freeze the protocol (budgets, repetitions, splits, metrics) before the final full run.
- [ ] Add confidence intervals or hierarchical/network-level uncertainty summaries.
- [ ] Verify negative-sampling choices against the target journal's norms and relevant literature.
- [ ] Update the manuscript's Results and Discussion from actual outputs only.
- [ ] Complete a literature review and novelty check against recent link-prediction evaluation work.
- [ ] Confirm the target journal's current scope, indexing, quartile and APC policy immediately before submission.
- [ ] Confirm authorship, affiliations, funding, competing interests and AI disclosure from real records.
