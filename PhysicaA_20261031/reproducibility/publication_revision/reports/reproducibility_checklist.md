## Archive scope

The code and data archives extract into the same `FCS_Reproducibility` directory. The full retained action-level observations for the recent studies are included in the data archive. The legacy WP0--WP9 compact archive includes source, locked configurations, derived tables and validation records. It does not contain every original historical propagation trajectory; recreating those simulations is different from recomputing the supplied derived summaries. The offline command does not mislabel the latter as a new raw simulation rerun.

The theoretical review is internal. Numerical validation and archive checks do not constitute independent human peer review or guarantee journal acceptance.
