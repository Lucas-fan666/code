# Scope of the refined guarantee

The original all-pairs Bonferroni construction can protect every internal
exact/approximate test simultaneously. The fixed-witness refinement protects
the returned epsilon-optimal action. It does not protect all M(M-1)
internal pairwise statements jointly at the same alpha. This is enough for
the original final-selection-quality gate; no epsilon, alpha or cost gate
is relaxed. Internal pairwise test outputs should not be repurposed as a
simultaneous confidence graph without their own multiplicity correction.

A proof witness is a fixed population minimizer, not an estimated incumbent.
Replacing it in the proof by a data-selected action without correction would
be invalid. The code does not perform that replacement.

For the three earlier unresolved records, retained inspection logs cross the
new threshold at 16064, 10976 and 14464 charged copies. This is an exploratory
check of the retained old schedules. The refined adaptive algorithm may
change elimination and later trajectories, so these are NOT simulations of
its stopping times or confirmatory coverage results. The new cohort, frozen
before its comparative outcomes, is the prospective test.
